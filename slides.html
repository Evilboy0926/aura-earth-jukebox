01 • Problem Statement

Earth Science is Visually Rich—But Acoustically Silent

The Accessibility Gap (2.2B+ People)

  • Over 2.2 billion people globally live with vision impairment or blindness (WHO).
  • NASA’s Earth Information Center (EIC) produces extraordinary satellite visualizations of climate change, but relying purely on color-coded maps locks out blind and low-vision learners.
  • Static alt-text cannot convey continuous spatial gradients or multi-frame temporal evolution.

Cognitive & Temporal Overload

  • Tracking simultaneous planetary shifts—such as sea surface warming in the Philippine Sea alongside tropical cyclone intensification—is difficult with sight alone.
  • The human auditory system excels at detecting subtle frequency shifts, stereo movement, and rhythmic deltas that the naked eye can miss across time-series frames.

02 • The Solution

Meet AURA-EARTH: The Browser-Native Earth Jukebox

AURA-EARTH is a zero-dependency web application that scans NASA EIC visualizations from West to East (`180°W` to `180°E`), synthesizing real-time polyphonic soundscapes directly from pixel colorbars.

1. Spatial Audio Radar

As the vertical scanline sweeps across the globe, Longitude maps smoothly to 3D stereo panning (`-1.0` Left Ear to `+1.0` Right Ear), letting listeners pinpoint anomalies geographically.

2. Musical Data Harmonies

Each vertical slice is divided into 16 latitude bands mapped to a 16-note C-Lydian scale (`C3` to `D5`)—eliminating harsh static noise in favor of meaningful harmonic chords.

3. Multi-Frame Time-Series

Users can upload sequential `.png`/`.jpg` frames from NASA SVS or run calibrated 4-frame sequences, hearing both spatial patterns and frame-to-frame temporal change ($\Delta$).

03 • NASA Data & Computer Vision Architecture

How Official NASA EIC & SVS Datasets Drive the Engine

STEP 1

NASA SVS EIC Frames

Loads single or sequential equirectangular frames from NASA SVS (IDs 5176, 4285, 5113, 5425) or built-in calibrated maps.

STEP 2

Canvas 2D Pixel Extraction

Extracts `1 x 400` vertical RGB slices via `getImageData()` on an offscreen buffer at up to 60 fps.

STEP 3

16-Band Colorbar Decoder

Applies dataset-specific scientific color transfer functions + frame-to-frame pixel Delta ($\Delta$) across 16 latitude bands.

STEP 4

Web Audio DSP & Export

Synthesizes 3 polyphonic voices + C2 drone through `BiquadFilterNode`, `StereoPannerNode`, `AnalyserNode`, & `MediaRecorder`.

4 Calibrated NASA SVS Scientific Profiles

  • SVS ID 5176 (SST Anomaly): Thermal red/orange marine heatwaves vs. cool blue baseline.
  • SVS ID 4285 (GPM Precipitation): Cyan/green rainbands & magenta/white cyclone cores.

High-Contrast Hazard & Current Profiles

  • SVS ID 5113 (VIIRS Active Fires): High-luminance orange/amber wildfire spikes over continents.
  • SVS ID 5425 (Perpetual Ocean 2): Kuroshio & Gulf Stream high-velocity boundary currents.

04 • Acoustic Mapping & Blind / Low-Vision Accessibility

Designed First for Screen Readers & Keyboard Exploration

Spatial & Harmonic Parameter Matrix

  • Longitude (180°W → 180°E): Continuous `StereoPannerNode` (`-1.00 L` to `+1.00 R`).
  • Latitude (90°S → 90°N): 16-Note C-Lydian Scale (`C3` 130.8 Hz to `D5` 587.3 Hz).
  • Anomaly Intensity (0–100%): Dynamic timbre (`Sine` → `Triangle` → `Sawtooth`) + Low-Pass Filter cutoff (`520 Hz` to `6000 Hz`).
  • Planetary Baseline: Subtle `C2` (`65.41 Hz`) equilibrium reference drone.

4-Tier Audio Legend & Spoken Telemetry

  • Keys `1`–`4` Reference Tones: Lets blind users audition `[1] Baseline (C3 Sine)`, `[2] Moderate (G3 Sine)`, `[3] Elevated (E4 Triangle)`, and `[4] Extreme (B4 Sawtooth)` before playback.
  • Key `N` Web Speech Narration: Speaks instant natural-language telemetry (active dataset, longitude, stereo pan, anomaly %, and musical note).
  • Arrow Key Scrubbing: `Left`/`Right` arrows step through longitudes manually with instant acoustic feedback.
